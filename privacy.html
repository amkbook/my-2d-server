Privacy Policy for 2D Pro

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the 2D Pro mobile application ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. 2D Pro is provided as an Ad-Supported, free application designed to display live 2D and 3D market results derived from the Stock Exchange of Thailand (SET) index.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. The App is an informational reference tool that does not require user registration or account creation, and does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or financial details.

1. Information Collection and Use

While using our Service, we do not require you to provide us with personally identifiable information. However, the App utilizes third-party services that may collect limited, non-personal technical information automatically to ensure proper app performance, analytics, and ad delivery:

  • Notifications: If you enable result alerts, a push notification token (e.g., via Firebase Cloud Messaging) is used to deliver real-time notifications to your device.
  • Crash & Diagnostic Data: Anonymous crash reports and performance metrics (such as device model, OS version, and app version) are processed via Firebase Crashlytics to help us fix bugs and improve stability.
  • Device & Usage Analytics: Anonymous metrics regarding app usage (such as screens visited and session durations) are processed via Google Analytics for Firebase to help us optimize user experience.
  • Advertising Identifier: The App uses Google AdMob to serve advertisements. AdMob may access your device’s Advertising ID to display and measure ads. You can reset or limit personalized advertising anytime via your device settings.

2. How We Use Information

We use the limited technical data collected solely for the following purposes:

  • To fetch and display real-time 2D/3D market results, history logs, and statistical tools.
  • To deliver push notification alerts that you have explicitly enabled.
  • To diagnose application crashes, monitor server stability, and optimize app performance.
  • To display relevant advertisements within the App.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

We strictly do not collect, store, sell, or share:

  • Personal identifiers (Name, Email, Phone Number, Home Address).
  • Precise GPS location data.
  • Contacts, Photos, Files, or Sensitive Device Permissions.
  • Financial records, banking details, or payment information.

4. Third-Party Services

The App incorporates third-party SDKs provided by Google. These third parties process non-personal data according to their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services / AdMob (Advertising)
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging & Analytics (Notifications & Performance Metrics)

You can review Google’s Privacy Policy here: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Data Retention & Your Choices

  • Notification Settings: You can enable or disable result alerts at any time within the App’s settings or through your phone's notification preferences.
  • Ad Personalization: You can opt out of personalized ads or reset your Advertising ID via your device settings (e.g., Settings → Google → Ads).
  • App Uninstallation: You can stop all data handling at any time simply by uninstalling the App from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

2D Pro is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App signifies your acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions, suggestions, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please feel free to contact us directly at:

Email: 2dproapk@gmail.com
